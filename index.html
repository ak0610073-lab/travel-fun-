    How to actually plan the trip

    How to reach, how many days you need, and roughly what it costs per person — for each of our signature journeys.

    Rajasthan

    The Desert Kingdom

    4–6 days
    ✈️
    How to reach
    Fly into Jaipur (JAI) from any metro; or overnight train from Delhi to Jaipur/Jodhpur/Jaisalmer.
    📅
    Best time
    October to March — summers cross 45°C in the desert.
    💰
    Cost per person
    ₹9,000–13,000Budget (hostels, trains, street food)
    ₹20,000–30,000Mid-range (3★ hotels, cabs)
    ₹45,000+Luxury (heritage havelis)
    Kerala

    The Backwaters

    5–7 days
    ✈️
    How to reach
    Fly into Kochi (COK); Alleppey and Munnar are 1.5–4hr drives from there.
    📅
    Best time
    September to March, post-monsoon greenery is at its best.
    💰
    Cost per person
    ₹11,000–15,000Budget
    ₹25,000–35,000Mid-range (shared houseboat)
    ₹50,000+Luxury (private houseboat)
    Ladakh

    The Cold Desert

    6–8 days
    ✈️
    How to reach
    Fly into Leh (IXL); spend 1–2 days acclimatising before heading to higher passes.
    📅
    Best time
    May to September — roads to Pangong/Nubra are snowbound the rest of the year.
    💰
    Cost per person
    ₹18,000–24,000Budget (shared cabs, guesthouses)
    ₹35,000–45,000Mid-range
    ₹65,000+Luxury (private jeep safari)
    Goa

    The Coastal Escape

    3–5 days
    ✈️
    How to reach
    Fly into Goa (GOI/Manohar); overnight trains from Mumbai/Bangalore also run daily.
    📅
    Best time
    November to February for cool, dry weather.
    💰
    Cost per person
    ₹6,000–9,000Budget (hostels, scooters)
    ₹15,000–22,000Mid-range
    ₹35,000+Luxury (beach resorts)
    Uttar Pradesh

    The Mughal Trail

    2–4 days
    ✈️
    How to reach
    Fast train from Delhi to Agra (2hrs) or Varanasi (flight, ~1.5hrs).
    📅
    Best time
    October to March, avoids the north-Indian summer heat.
    💰
    Cost per person
    ₹4,000–6,500Budget
    ₹10,000–16,000Mid-range
    ₹25,000+Luxury
    Meghalaya

    The Living Bridges

    4–6 days
    ✈️
    How to reach
    Fly into Guwahati (GAU), then a 3hr scenic drive to Shillong/Cherrapunji.
    📅
    Best time
    October to April — avoid peak monsoon (June–August) unless chasing the rains themselves.
    💰
    Cost per person
    ₹9,000–13,000Budget
    ₹18,000–26,000Mid-range
    ₹38,000+Luxury

    Hidden Gems
    Local Food Guide
    • Rajasthan — Kuldhara, an abandoned 13th-century village outside Jaisalmer, empty of tourists at sunset.
    • Kerala — Poovar's backwater-meets-sea inlet, quieter and cheaper than Alleppey.
    • Ladakh — Tso Moriri lake, less crowded than Pangong with the same colour.
    • Meghalaya — Krang Suri Falls, a turquoise pool locals swim in, rarely on itineraries.
    • Jaisalmer — Ker Sangri, a desert-bean dish almost never on restaurant menus, ask a homestay.
    • Kochi — Kallappam with mutton stew, a breakfast locals queue for before 8am.
    • Leh — Chutagi, a Ladakhi pasta-and-vegetable stew, warmer and heartier than momos.
    • Shillong — Tungtap, a fermented fish chutney that's a Khasi household staple.
